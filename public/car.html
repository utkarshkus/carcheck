How this answers

Answers come only from this car's manual. If the manual does not cover it, you get told that rather than a guess.

  • It cannot see your car's live data.
  • It will not give high voltage repair instructions.
  • Anything safety critical is sent to a service centre.

Section references point to the manual chapter so you can read the full warnings. A check marked "Not from the manual" is reasoning built on what the manual does say, not documented behaviour. Treat it as a hypothesis to test.
