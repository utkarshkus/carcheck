## Vehicle
- Tata Punch EV, micro SUV, 2024 onwards, on the acti.ev platform.
- Battery pack options: 25 kWh and 35 kWh.
- Instrument cluster sizes vary by variant: 4 inch, 7 inch and 10.25 inch. Display messages differ accordingly.

## Charging, home supply
- Use a 15 A socket. The manual's guidance where the charger reports "Error in domestic supply side" is to check the socket rating and use a 15 A socket. That error is raised when voltage is outside the acceptable range.
- The portable charger's control box indicator shows state:
  - Blinking: self inspection and standby states.
  - Solid: active charging.
  - Combined patterns: fault detection, covering plug temperature issues, supply errors and system malfunctions. The exact pattern for each fault is NOT in this extract.

## Service schedule items relevant to owners
- Every 6 months: clean the charging socket area properly, using compressed air to clear dust around the charging port housing lid.
- The schedule also covers Battery Cooling System (BCS) and Traction Cooling System (TCS) inspections and coolant changes, and diagnostic trouble code (DTC) checks. Intervals and procedures are NOT in this extract.
