# Mahindra XEV 9e manual extract

Condensed from the XEV 9e Owner's Manual (March 2025) and Quick Reference Vehicle Manual (February 2025), Mahindra Electric Automobile Limited. Section numbers in brackets are the manual's own. Features vary by variant (Pack One, Pack Two, Pack Three Select, Pack Three).

## Safety and high voltage [1.3, 1.6, 1.10]
- HV battery pack is approximately 307 V (59 kWh) or 409 V (79 kWh), sealed LFP Blade type.
- High voltage cables are orange. Never disassemble, remove or replace HV parts, cables or connectors. No user serviceable parts in the HV system.
- HV systems can be hot during charging, during driving and sometimes when switched off.
- The vehicle cannot be push started or tow started. Attempting it can damage the powertrain and motor.
- Only flatbed towing is permitted. [12.2]
- After a collision: avoid orange cables, damaged battery components and leaking fluids. Airbags can deploy even after the vehicle is stationary. The HV battery disconnects automatically in severe collisions. Exit and move to a safe distance. Tell roadside assistance it is an electric vehicle. Do not charge until inspected by a qualified Mahindra technician.
- Only class ABC fire extinguishers should be used.
- Do not carry out welding work on the vehicle outside a Mahindra authorised dealer.

## Battery care [1.3]
- Never allow the HV battery to fully discharge. Discharging to 0% may damage components including the 12 V battery. Repair and transport costs from deep discharge are the owner's liability and are not covered by warranty.
- Do not leave the vehicle at 100% SOC for prolonged periods.
- Do not expose the vehicle to very high or freezing temperatures for long.
- Do not use the HV battery as a stationary power source. This voids the warranty.
- Between minus 10 C and 0 C there is a considerable increase in charging time and a performance drop while driving.
- Battery performance decreases with time and usage. This does not indicate a defect.
- Charge in a well ventilated area away from flammable materials.

## Normal sounds [1.3]
- The HV contactor makes a "tak" noise when the vehicle is switched on or off.
- The traction motor makes a light sound in operation, louder during deceleration.
- The AC compressor makes sound while operating.
- During charging you may hear the fan, compressor, contactor and relay.
- Tyre and wind noise is more noticeable because the motor is quiet.

## Additional safety precautions [1.4]
1. Drive enable does not activate and the vehicle will not move unless the charge gun is unplugged from the charge port.
2. Apply the park brake before switching off the ignition and before leaving the vehicle.
3. Drive within 20 kmph over speed breakers and rough roads to avoid damage to the battery pack under the vehicle.
4. Tyres are tubeless and designed for all weather conditions.

## Specifications [2.6, 2.7]
- Motor: Interior Permanent Magnet Synchronous (IPMSM). Max power 170 kW or 210 kW. Max torque 380 Nm.
- Battery: 59 kWh or 79 kWh. Nominal voltage 307 V or 409 V.
- Chargers: EVSE 3 kW air cooled; wall box 7.2 kW and 11 kW air cooled; on-board charger 11 kW liquid cooled.
- Transmission: single speed. Steering: Electric Power Steering.
- Brakes: hydraulic with Integrated Electronic Booster, ABS and ESP. Front and rear disc. Electric Parking Brake.
- Tyres: 245/55 R19, optional 245/50 R20. Temporary spare T135/80 R18. Laden pressure front and rear 36 psi; temporary spare 60 psi.
- Wheel rims 8Jx19 or 8Jx20; spare 3.5Jx18.
- 12 V system, battery rating 55 Ah.
- Dimensions: wheelbase 2775 mm, length 4789 mm, width 1907 mm, height 1694 mm, track width 1630 mm.
- VIN is on a plate at the bottom of the driver side B pillar and punched below the co-driver seat. [2.8]

## Smart key remote [3.1]
- Two fob types: 6 button (with Auto Park) and 4 button (without). Buttons: unlock, lock, tailgate release, remote cooling, and on the 6 button fob forward and backward for Auto Park.
- Fob battery: CR2032, 3 V, 225 mAh. Positive side faces up when fitting. Open the cover with a small flat screwdriver; the emergency key blade is in the back case of the battery cover. [3.3.4]
- Inconsistent button response indicates a weak fob battery.
- Remote lock and unlock range is approximately 20 m. [3.1.13]
- Passive locking and unlocking needs the key within 0.7 m of the door handle (the manual also states not greater than 1 m / 3 ft).
- If remote range is reduced: stand closer in rain or bad weather; move left or right or hold the fob higher because vehicles or objects may block the signal; move away from radio station towers, airport transmitters, mobile or CB radios; check the fob battery.
- The fob may not work near radio stations or airports, when next to a mobile phone, laptop or tablet, when another vehicle's fob is operated close by, or when kept in the glove box or top bin. Keep the fob in the visible zone or in a pocket.
- Keep the fob away from water and liquids. Water damage is not covered by warranty.
- Do not place the fob on the wireless charger.
- Store the fob in an RFID blocking pouch to prevent relay attacks.
- Lost key [3.1.14]: report immediately to a Mahindra authorised service centre for deactivation. Maximum 2 keys per vehicle. Duplicates take a minimum of 10 days after formalities. Self made duplicates cannot deactivate the anti theft system or start the vehicle.
- Key fob limitations [3.1.15]: radio frequency interference from transformers or amateur radio can stop the fob working. It may start working again after moving out of the interference zone.

## Locking signals [3.1.3, 3.1.4, 3.1.10, 3.1.11, 3.1.12]
- Hazard lamps flash once on LOCK: all doors locked and armed.
- Hazard lamps flash five times with an alarm on LOCK: a door or the bonnet is open, or a fob is inside.
- Hazard lamps flash twice on UNLOCK: no theft attempt during the armed period. Front DRL and tail lamps also flash twice if equipped.
- Hazard lamps flash four times with an alarm on UNLOCK: there was a theft attempt while armed.
- Tailgate unlock alone: hazard lamps flash three times.
- Search function: press the tailgate button twice within 2 seconds. Hazards flash with an alarm for 30 seconds. Any fob button switches it off.
- Mute and unmute: press LOCK and UNLOCK together for 3 seconds.
- Theft deterrent alarm sounds the burglar horn and blinks the turn lamps for 27 seconds when someone manipulates the ignition or manually opens or unlocks the doors while armed. Press unlock on the remote or switch the ignition on with a valid key to disable it.
- If the vehicle is locked and armed and the driver door is opened with the key blade, the system treats it as unauthorised and triggers the alarm.
- Auto locking: all doors lock above 20 kmph. Auto unlocking: when ignition goes from ON to OFF, or in a crash on airbag variants. Auto re-locking: 45 seconds after unlocking with no door status change.
- If driver door unlock is enabled on the screen, one press of unlock opens the driver door only; press again for all doors.
- Passive lock while the ignition is ON with the fob outside triggers a chime and five hazard flashes, and a "Key fob Not Inside" warning if the driver leaves the vehicle.

## Walk Away Locking and Smart Approach Unlock [3.1.8]
- WAL: all doors, bonnet and tailgate closed and latched, and the user leaves with a valid fob within a 2 to 3 metre radius, then all doors lock automatically.
- SAU: approaching within 1 metre of the driver or co-driver door with a valid fob unlocks automatically.
- Enable or disable on the screen: App Drawer, Settings, My Vehicle, Exterior, Lock.
- If the vehicle is left locked and unattended for more than a week, SAU is disabled.
- If a fob is left inside the vehicle, WAL and SAU may not activate.
- After locking, keep the fob out of the 4 metre proximity zone or locking may fail or the car may unlock unintentionally.
- With a fob within 3 metres after locking, the key searching mechanism deactivates after 3 minutes to save battery, disabling SAU and WAL.
- Frequent use drains the fob battery faster. Fob battery wear is not covered by warranty.

## Doors, handles and child locks [3.3, 3.3.2]
- Front doors have flush handles, electric or manual depending on variant. Manual: push the handle once to make it pop out. Electric: the handle pops out automatically on unlock.
- Handles return to position when the vehicle is locked or vehicle speed exceeds 7 kmph.
- Key barrel for manual locking is on the driver door only. Insert the key blade and rotate clockwise to unlock, anti clockwise to lock. Do not apply abusive load.
- Locking or unlocking the driver door from outside or inside activates central locking for all doors.
- Rear doors use an integrated flap handle: insert four fingers behind the handle and press inward.
- Child safety rear door locks: push the lever down to activate, pull up to deactivate, until a distinct click. When engaged the rear doors cannot be opened from inside.
- Anti lock out: if the driver door is open the master actuator lock will not lock the vehicle.

## Power windows [3.4.1, 3.4.2, 3.4.4]
- Power windows operate when the ignition is ON, and for up to 3 minutes after the ignition is switched off (Extended Power Window).
- Do not operate power windows frequently with the ignition OFF as this drains the vehicle battery.
- If the switches are used often in a short period the system may become inoperable to prevent overheating. Wait 1 to 2 minutes without operating the switch.
- Thermal protection is tuned for 16 cycles before first activation; up movements are blocked for 30 seconds. Right after the 30 second inhibition, 2 cycles are available.
- Operate one window switch at a time.
- Power window lock switch on the driver door disables the rear passenger window switches.
- Smart power window (driver door, if equipped): auto up, auto down, and anti-pinch during auto up. Anti-pinch reverses the glass when an obstacle over 100 N is detected. Never test anti-pinch with a body part.
- Driver window can be opened or closed with the fob if equipped: hold lock for more than 1 second to close, unlock for more than 1 second to open.

## Power tailgate [3.1.6, 3.1.7, 3.1.9]
- Opening methods: driver side switch bank (short press opens, long press closes), fob tailgate release held 3 seconds, exterior switch on the inner side of the tailgate, kick sensor under the rear centre of the bumper, and the interior close switch.
- With the vehicle locked, the exterior switch and kick sensor require the fob within approximately 1 m. The tailgate will not open via kick input without the fob in either ignition state.
- Enable or disable the kick sensor: App Drawer, Vehicle Apps, My Vehicle, Exterior, Power Tailgate / Hands Free.
- Height presets: L1 is 60% of total height, L2 is 80%, L3 is 93%. Custom height can be programmed between 30% and 93%: open the tailgate, press and release the closing button at the height you want, then press and hold the closing button for 5 seconds until 3 buzzer beeps, then press and release the closing button again to close.
- Obstacle detection stops the tailgate or returns it to fully open when a severe impact or constant external force is applied. It may not work where resistance is insufficient or the tailgate is almost closed.
- If the power tailgate is operated 6 to 7 times consecutively the drive motor may be strained and the tailgate stops to protect the motor from overheating. Wait 1 minute or more.
- The power tailgate cannot be opened automatically with the ignition ON and vehicle speed of 5 km/h or more.
- Do not operate the power tailgate manually during ignition off as this affects spindle performance.
- Tailgate emergency release lever is at the bottom of the tailgate: open the cover, push the lever inside the trim panel to the right, and push the tailgate open.

## Hood and frunk [3.5, 3.6]
- Open: vehicle in Park, pull the lever below the steering wheel on the right side of the driver's seat twice.
- Close: from about 50 cm above the closed position, push down energetically with both hands, maintaining continuous pressure on both sides. If one side does not latch, press firmly on the green marked area of that side. Avoid the red marked areas, which can dent the hood or fender.
- Confirm latching: the hood is flush with the front spoiler, bumper grille and side fenders, and the driver display no longer indicates the hood is open.
- Front frunk maximum load is 60 kg, evenly distributed.

## Charge lid and charging [3.8, 3.8.1, 11.x, 13]
- Unlock the vehicle first, then gently press the marked dot on the charging lid to release and open it. Do not press elsewhere on the lid and do not force it.
- Wipe dust off the charge port with a soft cloth. Do not wash the charge port with water or chemicals.
- Charging lid manual override: the release cable is on the left hand side of the rear trim. Remove the safety cover and pull the release cable, then press the marked dot. Do not pull the cable with high force.
- Charge gun manual override: the release cable is in the left hand side quarter trim of the rear luggage compartment. Open the trunk, locate the trim cover, remove the safety cover, pull the black handled release cable, then gently remove the charger plug and close the lid. Confirm charging has stopped first. Never touch the metal connectors during manual override, which can cause burns or electric shock.
- A Charge Port Disconnect switch is on the driver side switch bank. [3.9.4]
- Do not force the connector. Pulling forcibly without the override can damage the port or connector.

### Charge port LED indications [11.4]
- White continuous: battery status.
- White blinking: system verification.
- Blue blinking: scheduled for charging.
- Red continuous: low battery, SOC below 10%.
- Red blinking: charge error.
- Amber blinking: gun stuck.
- Green animation: charging in progress.

### Charging equipment and supply [11.x]
- Home charging requires 3 kW single phase, 230 V AC, 16 A, a normal high quality electrical socket with proper earthing, a protected circuit breaker and a surge protection device.
- Socket wiring should be multi-stranded copper with a minimum cross section of 2.5 sq mm. Installation should be done by an authorised electrician.
- The 3 kW EVSE cable must not be used on a 5 A socket or an extension cord taken from a 5 A socket.
- Portable charger specification: input 230 V 50 Hz AC, output 230 V, max 13 A, 3 kW.
- Wall box 7.2 kW or 11 kW comes with selected variants only.
- Do not open, tamper with or modify the internal components of the charger. Inspect the cable, plug and socket for wear or damage before charging. Do not use worn, damaged or uncertified charging equipment. Do not store combustible materials near the charger.
- Fast charging: use CCS type 2 DC fast chargers only. 20% to 80% in about 20 minutes applies under optimal conditions on a 175 kW DC fast charger and depends on the battery pack variant. Charging duration varies with charger capacity, ambient temperature, battery condition and supply fluctuations.
- Charging time varies with state of charge, ambient and battery temperature, so any quoted time is an estimate.
- Normal and fast charging share a single CCS2 port located below the tail lamp on the left side.
- The 12 V auxiliary battery is charged automatically when the vehicle is in Ready to Drive mode or when the HV battery is charging.

## Starting, driving and brakes [6.x, 7]
- The Start/Stop button needs the smart key inside the vehicle.
- Shift by wire lever: press the brake pedal and move the lever to P, R, N or D. The selected gear is illuminated on the gear shifter.
- One Pedal Drive: press the one pedal button on the steering wheel. The vehicle comes to a stop when the accelerator is released, even without the brake. On steep hills press the brake pedal when stopped or parked.
- Regenerative braking: the paddle shifter adjusts regen from level 0 to 3. Left paddle (Regen +) increases regen and deceleration; right paddle (Regen minus) decreases it.
- Drive modes: Default, Range, Everyday, Race. The vehicle always starts in Default mode on every ignition cycle. Select via the drive mode button on the Intelli Command Center, or Menu, Vehicle Apps, My Vehicle, Drive Modes. Boost gives additional reserve power for 10 seconds and cannot be left on.
- Electric Parking Brake replaces the manual parking brake. Auto Vehicle Hold holds the vehicle at standstill with applied brake pressure and stays in memory once pressed. Do not rely solely on AVH on steep slopes, slippery roads or uneven surfaces.
- ABS: minimum speed for ABS to function is 12 kmph. Slight pulsation in the brake pedal and motor noise from the motor compartment are normal during ABS operation. Hold the brake pedal firmly rather than pumping it. ABS does not shorten stopping distance.
- If the ABS warning lamp does not go out after switching on, or comes on while driving, there is a fault in the ABS system. Normal braking remains efficient. Have the vehicle examined by a Mahindra authorised dealer as soon as possible.
- Wet brake discs reduce braking efficiency. After a wash or driving through water, apply and release the brake pedal mildly while driving to remove the water film.
- After long periods of non-usage a temporary drive disturbance may be felt. This is a tyre characteristic and should correct itself within 5 to 15 km. If it persists, have the vehicle checked immediately. [1.7]

## Immobiliser [3.2]
- The immobiliser prevents the ignition turning on or drive being enabled without the correct key. It activates automatically when the vehicle is locked with the fob after the ignition is off.
- If the immobiliser lamp flashes or remains continuously illuminated after the ignition is switched on, there is a system malfunction. Contact a Mahindra authorised service centre immediately.
- Do not modify, remove or disassemble the immobiliser. Tampering or third party components may render the vehicle inoperable and void warranty claims.

## Lamps [3.10.x]
- Lighting control stalk is on the right of the steering wheel. Outer rotary switch: OFF, head lamp, parking lamp, AUTO head lamp.
- Turn signals flashing faster than normal indicate one or more blown turn signal bulbs. Replace immediately.
- One Touch Lane Indicator: move the stalk to the limit of free movement and release, and the turn signals flash 3 times.
- Auto head lamps use the rain light sensor. On foggy, snowy, rainy or cloudy days use manual mode. Do not clean the sensor with detergent or wax. Aftermarket sun film or spray may cause the lighting system to malfunction. Tail and head lights may blink briefly when switching to AUTO, which is normal. The sensor may turn lamps on earlier than a person would expect, which is normal.
- Head lamps are vented and moisture may collect inside the lens under certain conditions. It evaporates after the head lamps are on for a few minutes.
- Head lamp levelling switch on the driver side switch bank has positions 0 to 3: 0 driver or driver with front passenger; 1 driver, front passenger and second row occupied; 2 all seats occupied; 3 all seats occupied with luggage, or driver with luggage at the extreme rear. Headlights can only be adjusted with low beam on.
- Front fog lamps only switch on when the parking lamps are on via park light input or auto light input. Rotate the inner rotary switch to the fog lamp icon.
- Auto Head Lamp Booster: in AUTO and high beam, above 80 kmph the high beam intensity and reach increase; it turns off automatically below 75 kmph.
- Cornering lamps turn on with ignition on, head lamp on and a sharp turn.
- Auto hazard: hazards turn on automatically when the bonnet is open with the vehicle stationary; for 10 seconds during panic braking; and for 30 minutes after airbag deployment, cancellable with the hazard switch or an ignition reset.
- Park lamp warning buzzer sounds if the park lamp is left on with the ignition off when the driver door is opened.
- Follow Me Home keeps the head lamp low beam on for about 20 seconds, extendable to a maximum of 3 minutes.

## Driver assistance and cameras [7, 8, 9]
- ADAS is Level 2 plus, with 5 radars and one camera. It is assistive only; the driver remains responsible at all times.
- ADAS features may include Adaptive Cruise Control, Speed Limit Assist, Rear Collision Warning, Collision Mitigation (AEB/FCW), Traffic Sign Recognition, Lane Keep Assist, Lane Centering, Smart Pilot Assist, High Beam Assist, Front Vehicle Start Alert, Blind Spot Detection, Rear Cross Traffic Alert, Door Open Alert, Highway Assist, Emergency Lane Keep Assist, Automatic Emergency Steering and Emergency Steering Assist.
- ADAS does not function reliably in fog, heavy rain or snow. Lane features fail where road markings are faded or obstructed. Performance may be affected on steep hills and in poor illumination. Dirty, obstructed or damaged sensors or cameras reduce detection. A dirty windshield affects performance.
- After an accident or impact affecting system performance, contact an authorised Mahindra service centre to check the front and corner radar units.
- Select Drive Assist: Menu, Vehicle Apps, Drive Assist.
- Parking Assist System works with the gear lever in D or R and warns via buzzer. Obstacles too close to the sensor may not be detected. False alarms can occur in heavy rain, snow and heavy wind.
- 360 degree camera uses 4 cameras in the front grille, rear bumper and both exterior mirrors. Keep lenses clean. Objects above camera height or outside the field of view are not displayed.
- VisionX head-up display visibility is influenced by driver seat position, dust or dirt on the protective glass, windshield dirt inside and outside, polarised sunglasses, wet road and unfavourable light. Adjust position and brightness via App Drawer, Vehicle Apps, VisionX, or the driver side switch bank push button. If the vision is unclear, have it checked.
- Driver Occupant Monitoring System (Eyedentity) monitors drowsiness, distraction and mobile usage. The in-car camera is always on to detect driver behaviour and user profile.

## Climate [10, 11]
- Climate control is operated through the Center Information Display: App Drawer, Vehicle Apps, Climate, Classic or Smart.
- Keep the system's air intake near the plenum free of snow, leaves and debris, and keep the area in front of the air vents clear inside the cabin.
- An air filter is fitted at the inlet of the blower and can be replaced.
- A PTC electric cabin heater heats the cabin. Cooling uses a vapour compression refrigeration cycle.
- Never service the climate control system yourself where refrigerant handling is involved. Self servicing voids the warranty. Refrigerant is a hazardous liquefied gas under high pressure; exposure can cause irritation and frostbite.
- Remote cooling can be started from the fob button or the Me4U app.
- Do not sleep in a parked vehicle with the windows closed and the air conditioner or heater on.

## Seats, mirrors and interior [3.9, 3.11, 3.12]
- Power driver seat with memory for up to three drivers. Setting: ignition on, adjust seat and ORVM, short press M, then press 1, 2 or 3 within 3 seconds, and wait for the chime. If a memory switch is not pressed within 3 seconds the position is not stored. Operating the ORVM control switch during setup cancels the memory setting.
- Restoring with ignition ON: press and hold a restore button to move the seat and ORVM. With ignition OFF, the seat alone restores. ORVM can only be recalled with the ignition on.
- During vehicle running, power seat movement is allowed for only 3 seconds per press.
- The manual also states the driver seat always moves to half of its remaining rearward travel, and that the final seat position may differ within an acceptable range depending on occupant weight and vehicle power supply status. This is normal and not a fault.
- Driver seat calibration, OWNER REPORTED and not in the manual. State that provenance if you cite it. With the car fully switched on, get out of the car, press and hold M and then, while still holding it, also hold the seat back slider, in that order, then release both. Calibration begins and the seat runs through its travel. Get back in, set the position and save it to a memory button again. Warn that the seat moves on its own, so keep people and objects clear of it. Use this when recall lands in the wrong position; if it still fails afterwards, send the owner to an authorised service centre.
- Wireless charging supports one Qi standard device. A metallic object such as a coin between the charger and phone stops charging and shows "Foreign object detected" on the cluster. Do not keep a debit or credit card between the phone and the charger. Misaligning the phone may prevent charging.
- USB: front row USB Type C; second row LH 65 W fast charge and RH data and charge 15 W if equipped. A 12 V power outlet is near the seat folding remote lever, and a second 12 V supply is in the boot.
- 12 V socket rating: 10 A maximum, which is 120 W at 12 V. OWNER REPORTED, not taken from the manual text in this extract. State that provenance if you cite it. An accessory drawing more than 10 A will blow the fuse. Many 12 V tyre inflators draw 10 to 15 A, so they sit at or above this limit.
- Do not leave roof or map reading lamps permanently on, which drains the vehicle battery.
- ORVM folds automatically when the doors are locked with the remote from outside, and unfolds when the ignition is turned on if it was folded and the switch is neutral. For auto fold the switch must be in the neutral position.

## Connected features, Me4U [1.13]
- Connected features require completed KYC. The mobile number given at KYC is the Me4U login.
- Subscription is free for a specified period and can be renewed from the app store. Without renewal only SOS eCall, RSA, Ask Mahindra, external charger malfunction alert, low charge alert and LV battery drain alert remain.
- External Charger Malfunction Alert: sent to the app if the external charger is faulty.
- Low Charge Alert: notifies the user when charge is low.
- LV Battery Drain Alert: alerts the user when the 12 V battery is discharging continuously. Contact an authorised Mahindra dealer for assistance.
- If the OTP is not received during login, use Resend OTP.

## Emergency contacts
- SOS: roof console button or the SOS icon on the screen. The eCall system notifies emergency services automatically in an accident, triggers a call to 108, and sends SMS with vehicle location to emergency contacts stored in the app.
- RSA: the RSA icon or the mobile app connects to the roadside assistance team.
- Ask Mahindra: connects to the Mahindra support centre.

## Virtual Engine Sound Simulators [6.6]
- VESS generates artificial sound so pedestrians hear the vehicle. Activate via App Drawer, Settings, Audio, Sonicsuite, Interior or Exterior, Sound ON or OFF.

## Tyres, spare and towing [1.6, 12.x, 14]
- Examine tyres for excessive or uneven tread wear, and check for stones, nails or glass in the tread and cuts or cracks in sidewalls. The customer should not attempt to remove lodged objects; have them checked by an authorised dealer.
- Never crawl under or place any part of your body under a vehicle supported solely by a jack.
- Tow hook provision is on the front and rear bumper. Remove the cap with a flathead screwdriver, then screw the tow hook and tighten clockwise.
- Only flat bed towing is allowed. Any other towing process may cause irreversible damage.
- A warning triangle, tool kit and spare wheel are provided; jack points are marked.

## Service schedule
Source: Warranty and Service Information Guide, XEV 9e, Mahindra Electric Automobile Limited, March 2025. This is a separate document from the owner's manual.

- First service at 2,000 km, then every 10,000 km.
- The first three services, at 2,000, 10,000 and 20,000 km, are labour-free. Consumables are chargeable at every service. Vehicle washing is complimentary.
- To keep warranty cover, all maintenance must be done at Mahindra authorised dealers using the recommended fluids and lubricants.
- Legend: I inspect and correct, R replace, L lubricate, CO carry out.

At the 2,000 km first service, 16 operations. One part is replaced, the cabin air filter. Everything else is inspection, lubrication or a carried-out task:
- Inspect: eTransmission oil; coolant level and leak; brake fluid level and leak; front and rear brake pads and calipers; ball joint and drive shaft boot for grease leak; HV battery bottom surface for damage; diagnostic trouble codes; LV 12 V battery health; all lights, horn, wipers and washer fluid; tyre pressure; pending FOTA update, checked and installed.
- Replace: HVAC particle filter, the cabin air filter. Chargeable.
- Lubricate: door hinges and check arm.
- Carry out: tyre rotation (chargeable), service reminder indicator reset, road test.
- Not done at 2,000 km: wheel alignment, wheel balancing, radiator clogging check.

Later intervals, up to 50,000 km:
- Wheel alignment: first at 40,000 km.
- Wheel balancing: from 40,000 km, if required.
- Radiator checked for clogging: from 30,000 km. Labour charged only if the radiator is clogged and must be removed and cleaned.
- eTransmission oil replaced at 20,000 km or 6 years, whichever is earlier.
- Brake fluid replaced at 50,000 km or 3 years, whichever is earlier.
- Coolant replaced at 120,000 km or 6 years, whichever is earlier.
- The HVAC particle filter is replaced at every service.
- Tyre rotation is carried out at every service and is labour-chargeable in its own right.
- The published chart runs to 200,000 km. If asked about an interval beyond 50,000 km other than the replacement points listed above, say the site holds the schedule only to 50,000 km and point to the guide.
